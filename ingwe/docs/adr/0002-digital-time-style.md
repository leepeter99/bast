# Digital time style, with outlined hands in always-on mode

Digital is a third time style: Hybrid's stacked hours, minutes and seconds right of centre, with the right field and the two right corner bars dropped (Ingwe ADR 0003), but no hour, minute or seconds hands and no centre hub. In always-on mode it still shows the 1 px outlined hands (Pulse ADR 0003), not the digits, so a Digital wearer sees hands only while the watch sleeps. That's deliberate: always-on mode is the same in every time style, and the large digits would light far more pixels than 1 px outlines.
