# Context Map

## Contexts

- [Pulse](./pulse/CONTEXT.md): analog face whose three fields relocate out of the hands' way
- [Ingwe](./ingwe/CONTEXT.md): analog, hybrid or digital face whose four fields stay at their homes

## Relationships

- **Ingwe → Pulse**: Ingwe borrows Pulse's language for corner bars, bar metrics, data and modes (Corner bar, Move alert, Rest, Air quality, Recovery peak, Unavailable, Always-on mode and the rest). Only layout and time style differ, and those terms are defined in Ingwe's own glossary.
