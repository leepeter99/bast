bast_pulse is an analog watch face for the Forerunner 965 with data fields that move out of the way of the hands.

Four circular gauges sit at the top, right, bottom and left of the dial. When the hour or minute hand passes over a gauge, that gauge moves to a free spot so the reading stays visible. Each gauge expands into the space around it, and fields with a natural range, such as battery or Body Battery, show a progress arc.

FIELDS (pick any four):
• Heart rate
• Resting heart rate
• Recovery time
• Body Battery
• Stress
• Steps
• Calories
• Distance (km or mi, from your device settings)
• Move alert
• Watch battery
• Date with weekday
• Weather
• Sunrise
• Second time zone: London, New York, Tokyo, Sydney, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Kolkata, Dubai or UTC, with daylight saving applied. You can also set a custom UTC offset with an optional 3-letter label.

CUSTOMIZE:
• Chapter ring: Compass (decorative N/E/S/W and degree labels), Hour numerals or Ticks only
• Accent colour: Vibranium purple, Deep violet, Soft lavender, Amber, Ice Blue or Lume Green

ALWAYS-ON:
In low-power mode, the face shows only outlined hour and minute hands, shifted slightly each minute to protect the AMOLED display.

If a reading isn't available, its gauge shows "--".
