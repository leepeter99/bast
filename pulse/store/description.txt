Bast Pulse is an analog watch face for AMOLED Garmin watches with three data gauges that move out of the way of the hands, and four segmented corner bars along the edge for your daily progress.

GAUGES
Three large circular gauges sit at the top, lower right and lower left of the dial. When the hour or minute hand passes over a gauge, it moves to the free spot where it can stay biggest, so the reading stays visible. If there is no room, the gauge hides until the hands move on. Each gauge grows as large as the hands and its neighbours allow, and its text grows with it. Fields with a natural range, such as watch battery, show a progress arc.

Pick any three of:
• Heart rate
• Resting heart rate
• Recovery time
• Weather
• Watch battery
• Date with weekday
• Calories
• Distance (km or mi, from your device settings)
• Sunrise / Sunset (shows whichever is next)
• Stress
• Pulse Ox
• Respiration
• High / low temperature
• Weekly bike distance
• Weekly run distance
• Altitude
• Barometric pressure
• Second time zone: London, New York, Tokyo, Sydney, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Kolkata, Dubai or UTC, with daylight saving applied. You can also set a custom UTC offset with an optional 3-letter label.

CORNER BARS
Four bars run along the dial edge. Each one shows Steps, Body Battery, Move alert, Recovery, Stress or Floors climbed, or can be turned off. The fill always tracks the real value.
• Each bar shows a Black Panther icon and its number, such as a paw print and your full step count.
• Move alert reads as vibranium charge: full and purple, labelled CHARGED, when you've moved recently. It dims a segment for each step of idleness and recharges when you walk it off.
• Recovery fills as your recovery time runs down, and shows RECHARGED at zero. It is also available as a gauge.
• Stress fills as you calm down and turns orange when stress is high. It is also available as a gauge.

CUSTOMIZE
Accent colour: Vibranium purple, Deep violet, Soft lavender, Amber, Ice Blue or Lume Green. Change gauges and bars from the phone app or on the watch.

ALWAYS-ON
In low-power mode, the face shows only outlined hour and minute hands, shifted slightly each minute to protect the AMOLED display.

COMPATIBLE WATCHES
Forerunner 965 and 970, fēnix 8 AMOLED (43mm, 47mm, Pro 47mm) and fēnix 9 AMOLED (43mm, 47mm, Pro 43mm, 47mm, 51mm). The layout scales automatically to each screen size. Solar (MIP display) models are not supported.

If a reading isn't available, it shows "--".
