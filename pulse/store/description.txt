bast_pulse is an analog watch face for AMOLED Garmin watches with data gauges that move out of the way of the hands, and four segmented corner bars along the edge for your daily progress.

GAUGES
Four large circular gauges sit at the top, right, bottom and left of the dial. When the hour or minute hand passes over a gauge, it moves to a free spot so the reading stays visible. Each gauge grows as large as the hands and its neighbours allow, and its text grows with it. Fields with a natural range, such as watch battery, show a progress arc.

Pick any four of:
• Heart rate
• Resting heart rate
• Weather
• Watch battery
• Date with weekday
• Calories
• Distance (km or mi, from your device settings)
• Sunrise / Sunset (shows whichever is next)
• Stress
• Pulse Ox
• Respiration
• High / low temperature
• Weekly bike distance
• Weekly run distance
• Altitude
• Barometric pressure
• Second time zone: London, New York, Tokyo, Sydney, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Kolkata, Dubai or UTC, with daylight saving applied. You can also set a custom UTC offset with an optional 3-letter label.

CORNER BARS
Four bars run along the dial edge. Each one shows Steps, Body Battery, Move alert, Recovery, Stress or Floors climbed, or can be turned off. The fill always tracks the real value.
• Move alert reads as vibranium charge: it glows CHARGED when you've moved recently, dims a segment for each step of idleness, and recharges when you walk it off.
• Recovery fills as your recovery time runs down, and shows RECHARGED at zero. It is also available as a gauge.
• Stress fills as you calm down and turns orange when stress is high. It is also available as a gauge.

TIME STYLE
• Analog: hands only.
• Hybrid: stacked hour, minute and second digits to the right of centre, under the hands, with three gauges. 12 or 24 hour format, or follow your watch setting.

CUSTOMIZE
Accent colour: Vibranium purple, Deep violet, Soft lavender, Amber, Ice Blue or Lume Green.

ALWAYS-ON
In low-power mode, the face shows only outlined hour and minute hands, shifted slightly each minute to protect the AMOLED display.

COMPATIBLE WATCHES
Forerunner 965 and 970, fēnix 8 AMOLED (43mm, 47mm, 51mm, Pro) and fēnix 9 AMOLED (43mm, 47mm, 51mm, Pro). The layout scales automatically to each screen size. Solar (MIP display) models are not supported.

If a reading isn't available, it shows "--".
